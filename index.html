Travel videos, posted on time.

Stella Visit Scheduler helps the creator behind @stella.visit share travel videos from Italy on TikTok, with a short caption for each one, at the times the creator chooses.

How it works

  • The creator signs in with TikTok and allows the app to post videos to their own account.
  • The creator selects the videos and the posting times, and reviews the captions.
  • At the chosen time the app uploads the video to TikTok through TikTok's official Content Posting API.
  • The creator can stop posting at any time and can remove the app's access in the TikTok app.

What it does not do

  • It does not read or change anything else on your TikTok account.
  • It does not sell or share your data, and it shows no ads.
  • It does not post to accounts that have not signed in and given permission.
