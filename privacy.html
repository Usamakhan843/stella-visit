Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what information Stella Visit Scheduler ("the app", "we") uses when you connect your TikTok account, why, and the choices you have. The app is operated by Usama, an individual creator, to publish travel videos to TikTok accounts that have signed in and given permission.

Information we use

  • Basic TikTok account information provided by TikTok when you sign in (the user.info.basic permission): your account's unique ID (open_id), display name and avatar. We use it only to show which account is connected and to post to the right account.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok so the app can post on your behalf (the video.publish permission).
  • Posting information returned by TikTok, such as the publish ID and status of each upload, and the privacy options TikTok offers for your account, so the app can confirm a post was published.
  • The videos and captions you choose to post. The app uploads only the videos selected by the account owner.

We do not collect your TikTok password, contacts, messages, followers, viewing history or any other data from your account.

How we use it

  • To publish the videos you chose, at the times you chose, with the captions you approved.
  • To check whether each post was published successfully.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and we never sell it.

Where it is stored and for how long

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the operator's own computer and are never published or shared. Posting records (which video was posted, when, and its TikTok publish ID) are kept so the same video is not posted twice. Videos are kept only until they have been uploaded and are then deleted from the app's working copy. When you disconnect the app, its tokens are deleted.

Sharing

Information is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to publish your videos. Captions may be drafted with the help of an AI writing service, which receives a still image from the video and no TikTok account data. We do not share your information with anyone else, except where required by law.

Your choices

  • Disconnect at any time: in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions (or Apps and services permissions), and remove Stella Visit Scheduler. The app can then no longer post.
  • Deletion: email us at the address below and we will delete any records about your account within 30 days.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly process information about children.

Changes
